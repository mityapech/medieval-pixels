        How to play

        Your town lives on its own: about a hundred people in families, each with a trade, a home and a day of work. You watch it grow, prosper or struggle over the years. Your town is saved in this browser and carries on where you left it.

        • Walk around with W A S D or the arrow keys; zoom with + and −.
        • Speed: Pause, 1×, 10× or 60× at the top. Step one day skips to tomorrow.
        • Click a person to see their family, work, coins and today's tasks; click a building for its owner, rent and history.
        • Town Hall: the mayor, their doctrine and what they are changing. Elections come every two years, with campaigns at the market.
        • Ledger: charts of your town's history, from the day it was founded.
        • Bottom left: conversations near you, town news, and the market's notices.

        Settings

        WASD or arrow keys to walk · click anyone or any building to inspect · + / − to zoom
